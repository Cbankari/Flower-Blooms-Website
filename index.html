    About Us



    Founded in 1923, Flower Blooms carries forward a heritage of beauty and craftsmanship, standing as one of the world’s leading names in floral gifting and décor. With a network of skilled floral designers spanning over 100 countries, our brand is synonymous with elegance, creativity, and heartfelt expression. Flower Blooms India is the story of a legacy reimagined for a modern, discerning audience. Since our launch in India in 2017 under the visionary leadership of Anuja Joshi, we’ve set out to create more than just floral arrangements — we’ve designed an entire floral lifestyle experience. Our aim has been to bring excellence into décor, design, and gifting, making every occasion truly unforgettable. As India’s first luxe floral brand, our journey was filled with challenges — from the absence of an efficient supply chain to inconsistent flower quality and short shelf life. We began from scratch, onboarding and training local florists, collaborating with over 200 growers, exploring untapped flower-growing regions, and introducing rare and exquisite floral varieties across the country. Over time, we developed a seamless supply chain to ensure every arrangement arrives in its finest form. At Flower Blooms, we believe success lies in attention to detail. Every bouquet, hamper, and décor design is a reflection of our commitment to artistry and perfection.


    Mission Statement
    To combine handcrafted floral artistry with modern innovation, helping people celebrate life’s most cherished moments with unmatched floral elegance.

    Vision Statement
    To be India’s most trusted and admired luxury flower gifting brand, known for exceptional quality and service.
    Today, Flower Blooms serves customers in over 25 cities across India, powered by a team of 180+ internationally trained floral designers, 100 specialized floral studios, and a strong logistics network that guarantees on-time delivery, every time.
